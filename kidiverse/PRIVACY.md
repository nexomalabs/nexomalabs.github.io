*Kidiverse collects nothing about your child. This page explains exactly what that means.*

## The short version

Kidiverse is made by Nexoma Labs LLC. It is a game in which a child plays by moving their body, and it uses the device’s camera to do that. This policy tells you precisely what happens to what the camera sees, and to everything else.

Nothing the camera sees is recorded, saved or uploaded, and there is no account. Kidiverse keeps three small things, on your device and nowhere else: the stars your child has earned, the No hops setting, and a short list of problems if part of the app stops working. Kidiverse has no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Everything below is the long version.

## For children

Kidiverse uses the camera so that a character on the screen can copy how you move. Your picture is never shown, and nothing the camera sees is recorded or kept. The stars you earn stay on this phone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. If you want to know more, ask a grown-up to read this page with you.

## What Kidiverse collects

Nothing.

Kidiverse sends nothing to us, so we receive nothing about your child. There is no sign-up, no account, no email address, no name, no age, no date of birth, no device identifier, no advertising identifier and no location. Kidiverse never asks a child or a parent to type anything about themselves, because there is nowhere to type it.

There is no analytics, no usage tracking, no crash reporting, no advertising and no marketing measurement in the app. These are not switched off. They are not in the app. If part of Kidiverse stops working, it writes a short note about it on your device, described below, and never sends that note anywhere itself.

## What the camera does

This is the part that matters, so it gets its own section. Kidiverse shows you these same words before your device asks for camera permission.

Your child plays by moving, and a character on screen moves with them. Kidiverse uses the camera to follow your child’s body — arms, legs, how they move — and a drawn character copies them: a stick figure, our mascot, or one of the drawn characters your child picks. Their picture is never put on the screen. The camera sees the whole room, as any camera does, and this phone uses what it sees, moment to moment, to follow the movement.

If someone else walks into view, the character may follow them instead. The camera cannot tell one person from another. It follows one body at a time, so a grown-up or a brother or sister walking past can take over the character for a moment. Their picture is not shown either. To leave the game to your child, stay out of the camera’s view.

Nothing is recorded. Nothing is saved. Nothing is sent anywhere. What the camera sees is used on this phone and then it is gone. There is no recording, no photo, no video file, and nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else. Closing the app leaves nothing behind.

You can say no, and you can stop at any time. If you decline the camera, Kidiverse cannot run — the whole game is your child moving. Nothing else in the app changes, and you can grant it later from your device settings.

## Where the processing happens

All of it happens on your device. The camera’s frames are read by the phone to find where the body’s joints are, and the character is drawn from those positions. No frame is kept once it has been read.

The joint positions are used moment to moment, to draw the character and to recognise moves such as a jump, and are then discarded. They are never stored or sent, and Kidiverse does not use them, or anything else, to recognise who a person is. It keeps no fingerprint, face, voice, gait or other biometric record of anyone.

The part of Kidiverse that handles the camera links no networking software at all. It has no way to open a connection, so it has no way to send a picture anywhere, whether or not anyone intended it to. This is checked automatically every time the code is tested.

Nexoma Labs LLC operates no server for Kidiverse. Kidiverse needs no internet connection, and there is nowhere for your child’s data to be sent even in principle.

## Playing on a TV

Kidiverse can be played on a television using your phone’s own screen mirroring: AirPlay on an iPhone, or Cast, Screen cast or Smart View on Android. A grown-up starts it from the phone’s system controls; Kidiverse cannot start it, and only shows the steps.

The TV shows exactly what the phone’s screen shows: a drawn character copying the child, never their camera picture. The picture travels from your phone to your TV over your home network, sent by your phone’s operating system, not by Kidiverse. Kidiverse itself records nothing and sends nothing. To stop, turn mirroring off from the same controls.

## What Kidiverse keeps on your device

Three small files, and nothing else. Kidiverse never sends any of them to us or to anyone else.

**The stars your child has earned.** This file holds the total number of stars, how many rounds have been played, and which moves and challenges have been done at least once, such as “jumped in the Snow world”. It does not hold a name, an age, a picture, a recording, any body measurement, or the time or date of any game. It is a running total, not a history of each day’s play. To delete it, open Settings and choose Clear stars.

**The No hops setting.** One switch a grown-up can turn on in Settings, for a child who should not jump: every world then asks for a knee lift instead of a hop. The file holds only whether the switch is on or off.

**A short list of problems.** If part of Kidiverse stops working, your child is taken back to the map and the app writes down what went wrong, so that it can be fixed. Each note says when it happened (to the minute), which part of the app was on screen, and the error the app’s own code reported. It holds nothing about your child: no name, no picture, nothing the camera saw, and nothing about how they moved. Only the ten most recent problems are kept. You can read the list in Settings, under Problems on this device, and clear it there. Kidiverse never sends it; if you want us to see it, you can email it to us from your own mail app, and you can read or change the message before you send it.

On Android these files are left out of Google’s cloud backup. On an iPhone, a backup you make of your own phone, to iCloud or to a computer, includes them as it includes other apps’ files; that backup is yours and is not something Kidiverse sends. Deleting the app deletes all three.

Kidiverse keeps no picture, recording or history of play on the device. Your device itself remembers whether you allowed the camera, as it does for every app, and you can change that in your device settings at any time.

## Permissions

Kidiverse asks you for one permission: the camera. It explains why before your device asks.

Kidiverse does not ask for the microphone, photos, contacts, location, health data, motion data or notifications, and it does not use them.

On Android, the app’s store listing and system settings also show permissions you are never asked for. Android grants them without a question when the app is installed, and some are added automatically by the software Kidiverse is built with. Kidiverse itself declares two: permission to change audio settings, which lets it control how its own sounds play, and Google Play's billing permission, which lets a grown-up buy Kidiverse Plus through Google Play. It does not give access to the microphone and cannot record anything. None of these permissions is used to collect anything about your child or to send anything from your device.

## Children’s privacy

Kidiverse is made for children to play, with a grown-up nearby. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 in the United States or children under the age of digital consent where you live. Because nothing is collected, there is no personal information to ask parental consent for, and none to share, sell, keep or delete.

In particular, Kidiverse uses no persistent identifier (such as an advertising ID or device ID), does not use the microphone or record anyone’s voice, and gives a child no way to make any information about themselves public.

Kidiverse has no social features, no chat, no accounts and no user-generated content. The only thing to buy is Kidiverse Plus, an optional subscription that is offered only in the settings, behind the parental gate, with a second gate question before the store's payment sheet. Nothing a child sees while playing mentions it.

When a grown-up buys or restores Kidiverse Plus, the purchase is made with Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, using the store account already on the device. Kidiverse asks the store only whether this device has Plus. It keeps no record of the answer, and sends nothing about the purchase, or about your child, to us or to anyone else. The settings, these pages and every link that leaves the app sit behind a parental gate: a multiplication question that has to be answered on a keypad. After three wrong answers the gate locks for a while, and the wait grows each time, so a young child cannot get through by guessing.

## Sharing, selling and tracking

Kidiverse shares nothing. The stars your child earns never leave your device, and the list of problems leaves it only if you email it to us yourself. Nexoma Labs LLC does not sell, rent, trade or disclose information about your child or about you to anyone. There are no advertising partners, no analytics providers, no data brokers and no third-party software in the app that collects data.

Kidiverse does not track you across other apps or websites. Because it does no tracking of any kind, a Do Not Track signal changes nothing about how it behaves.

## If you contact us

This is the only personal information we ever receive: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and the app version and system version if you leave them in. If you send us the list of problems from Settings, we also receive that list, which is described above and says nothing about your child.

We use it only to reply to you and to fix what you tell us about. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not share your message with anyone other than the email provider that delivers and stores it. We keep it only as long as we need it for the question you raised.

Where the law asks for a reason we may do this, it is our legitimate interest in answering you, which you started by writing to us.

## How long we keep information

Kidiverse collects no personal information from children, so we hold none, at any time, and there is none to keep or delete. The stars, the No hops setting and the list of problems are on your device, not with us, and they stay there until you clear them or delete the app.

The only personal information we ever receive is an email someone chooses to send us. We keep a message from a parent or other adult only as long as we need it for the question it raised, and then delete it.

If a message comes from a child, we use their email address only to reply to that one message. We never contact them again, never share their address or message with anyone, and delete both promptly after replying.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. For Kidiverse that can only ever be an email you sent us. Write to support@nexomalabs.com.

If you are a parent and think your child has emailed us, write to the same address. You can ask to see what your child sent, ask us to delete it, and tell us not to reply to or contact your child. We do what you ask, and we never use a child’s email address for anything other than answering their message.

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law, we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

## How to delete your data

Everything Kidiverse keeps is on your device, and you can delete each part from Settings in the app (behind the parental gate):

- **Stars:** choose Clear stars. This cannot be undone.
- **List of problems:** open Problems on this device and choose to clear it.
- **No hops:** turn the switch off.

To remove Kidiverse entirely, delete the app from your device in the normal way, and all of it goes with it.

If you have emailed us and want that message deleted, ask at support@nexomalabs.com and we will delete it.
