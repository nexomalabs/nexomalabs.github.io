# Privacy Policy

*Kidiverse collects nothing about your child. This page explains exactly what that means.*

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026

## The short version

Kidiverse is made by Nexoma Labs LLC. It is a game in which a child plays by moving their body, and it uses the device’s camera to do that. This policy tells you precisely what happens to what the camera sees, and to everything else.

Nothing is recorded, nothing is saved, nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. Kidiverse has no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Everything below is the long version.

## For children

Kidiverse uses the camera so that a character on the screen can copy how you move. Your picture is never shown. Nothing is recorded or kept, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. If you want to know more, ask a grown-up to read this page with you.

## What Kidiverse collects

Nothing.

There is no sign-up, no account, no email address, no name, no age, no date of birth, no device identifier, no advertising identifier and no location. Kidiverse never asks a child or a parent to type anything about themselves, because there is nowhere to type it.

There is no analytics, no usage tracking, no crash reporting, no advertising and no marketing measurement in the app. These are not switched off. They are not in the app.

## What the camera does

This is the part that matters, so it gets its own section. Kidiverse shows you these same words before your device asks for camera permission.

Your child plays by moving, and a character on screen moves with them. Kidiverse uses the camera to follow your child’s body — arms, legs, how they move — and a drawn character copies them: a stick figure, our mascot, or a skeleton. Their picture is never put on the screen. The camera sees the whole room, as any camera does, and this phone uses what it sees, moment to moment, to follow the movement.

If someone else walks into view, the character may follow them instead. The camera cannot tell one person from another. It follows one body at a time, so a grown-up or a brother or sister walking past can take over the character for a moment. Their picture is not shown either. To leave the game to your child, stay out of the camera’s view.

Nothing is recorded. Nothing is saved. Nothing is sent anywhere. What the camera sees is used on this phone and then it is gone. There is no recording, no photo, no video file, and nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else. Closing the app leaves nothing behind.

You can say no, and you can stop at any time. If you decline the camera, Kidiverse cannot run — the whole game is your child moving. Nothing else in the app changes, and you can grant it later from your device settings.

## Where the processing happens

All of it happens on your device. The camera’s frames are read by the phone to find where the body’s joints are, and the character is drawn from those positions. No frame is kept once it has been read.

The part of Kidiverse that handles the camera links no networking software at all. It has no way to open a connection, so it has no way to send a picture anywhere, whether or not anyone intended it to. This is checked automatically every time the code is tested.

Nexoma Labs LLC operates no server for Kidiverse. Kidiverse needs no internet connection, and there is nowhere for your child’s data to be sent even in principle.

## What Kidiverse keeps on your device

Nothing about your child. Kidiverse keeps no progress, history, log, picture or recording on the device, and each time it opens it has no memory of earlier play.

Your device itself remembers whether you allowed the camera, as it does for every app. You can change that in your device settings at any time.

If a future version keeps progress on the device, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and will say exactly what is kept and how to clear it.

## Permissions

The only permission Kidiverse asks you for is the camera, and it explains why before your device asks.

Kidiverse does not ask for the microphone, photos, contacts, location, health data, motion data or notifications, and it does not use them.

## Children’s privacy

Kidiverse is made for children to play, with a grown-up nearby. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 in the United States or children under the age of digital consent where you live. Because nothing is collected, there is no personal information to ask parental consent for, and none to share, sell, keep or delete.

Kidiverse has no in-app purchases, no social features, no chat, no accounts and no user-generated content. The settings, these pages and every link that leaves the app sit behind a parental gate: a two-digit multiplication that has to be typed, which a young child cannot answer and cannot guess.

## Sharing, selling and tracking

Kidiverse shares nothing, because it has nothing to share. Nexoma Labs LLC does not sell, rent, trade or disclose information about your child or about you to anyone. There are no advertising partners, no analytics providers, no data brokers and no third-party software in the app that collects data.

Kidiverse does not track you across other apps or websites. Because it does no tracking of any kind, a Do Not Track signal changes nothing about how it behaves.

## If you contact us

This is the only personal information we ever receive: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and the app version and system version if you leave them in.

We use it only to reply to you and to fix what you tell us about. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not share your message with anyone other than the email provider that delivers and stores it. We keep it only as long as we need it for the question you raised.

Where the law asks for a reason we may do this, it is our legitimate interest in answering you, which you started by writing to us.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. For Kidiverse that can only ever be an email you sent us. Write to support@nexomalabs.com.

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law, we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

## How to delete your data

There is nothing in the app to delete, because nothing is kept. To remove Kidiverse entirely, delete the app from your device in the normal way, and everything associated with it goes too.

If you have emailed us and want that message deleted, ask at support@nexomalabs.com and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If Kidiverse changes what it does with data, this page is updated before the changed version is released, and the date at the top changes with it. A change that means Kidiverse begins keeping or sending something will be said at the top of this page in plain words, not folded into a paragraph.
