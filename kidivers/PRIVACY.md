# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 27, 2026**

**Nexoma Labs LLC** ("Nexoma Labs", "we", "us", "our") builds and operates **Kidivers** (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles, why we handle it, who it is shared with, and what control you have over it. It applies to the Kidivers mobile application and any related services we provide.

We wrote this in plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, email support@nexomalabs.com and we will explain it.

## 1. Summary

- **Kidivers is built for children, and it is designed to collect as little as possible.**
- **We do not collect personal information from children.** There is no account, no sign-in, no profile and no free-text sharing.
- **There is no third-party advertising, no in-app advertising network, and no cross-app tracking.**
- **There is no analytics SDK** and no third-party software development kit that profiles users.
- **Purchases and external links, where they exist, sit behind a parental gate** that a young child cannot pass.
- **We do not sell personal information**, and we never have.

## 2. Information We Collect

The table below lists every category of information the App can handle, and why.

| Category | Examples | Where it is stored | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Progress and settings | Level reached, stars or badges earned, sound and music toggles, chosen difficulty | On the device only | To resume where the child left off |
| Parent settings | Parental gate result, purchase state | On the device only | To keep restricted areas restricted |
| Diagnostic data | Crash logs, performance data, device model, operating system version | Apple, if the device owner opted in to share analytics with developers | To find and fix crashes and defects |
| Support correspondence | A parent's email address and the contents of messages sent to us | Our email provider | To answer the question and keep a record of the exchange |

None of the progress or settings data identifies a child. It is not linked to a name, an email address, a device advertising identifier or any persistent cross-app identifier.

### No Registration, No Sharing

Kidivers does not ask a child to register, create a profile, enter a name, upload a photo, or type free-form text that could be transmitted. There is no chat, no comment feature and no social sharing inside the child experience.

### Parental Gate

Anything that leaves the child experience is placed behind a parental gate that requires an adult-level action to pass. This includes in-app purchases, links to external websites, links to social media, and our support email address.


## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and the App does not ask for:

- Your precise or background location.
- Your contacts, calendars, photos or microphone, unless you explicitly grant that permission for a feature you have chosen to use, in which case the data is used only for that feature.
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA). The App does not run third-party advertising and does not participate in cross-app tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.
- Payment card numbers or bank details. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment instrument.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

- Provide the features of the App and store the data you create in it.
- Diagnose crashes and fix defects.
- Respond to your support requests.
- Meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

## 5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract** to provide the App's core features to you.
- **Legitimate interests** to keep the App secure, stable and free of abuse, balanced against your rights.
- **Consent** for any optional permission you grant, such as notifications. You may withdraw consent at any time in your device settings.
- **Legal obligation** where we must retain records to comply with law.

## 6. Sharing and Disclosure

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.** We have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

We disclose information only in these situations:

- **Service providers.** Vendors that host or operate parts of our infrastructure may process data on our instructions, under contract, and only to provide their service to us.
- **Legal requirements.** If required by valid legal process, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of Nexoma Labs, our users, or the public.
- **Business transfer.** If Nexoma Labs is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction. We will give notice before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Kidivers is intended for children, and we treat this section as the most important part of this policy.

**Compliance.** We operate the App in accordance with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and its implementing rule, the requirements of Apple's Kids Category, and, where applicable, the EU and UK GDPR provisions on children's data and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

**What this means in practice:**

- We do **not** knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from children under 13.
- We do **not** require a child to disclose more information than is reasonably necessary to use the App.
- We do **not** include third-party advertising, behavioural advertising, or any advertising network in the App.
- We do **not** include third-party analytics, attribution or marketing software development kits.
- We do **not** create persistent identifiers that track a child across apps or websites.
- We do **not** condition a child's participation in an activity on disclosing personal information.

**Parents and guardians.** Because we do not collect personal information from children, there is normally nothing for us to disclose or delete. If you believe your child has nonetheless provided personal information to us, email **support@nexomalabs.com** with the subject line "COPPA request". We will verify that you are the parent or guardian, tell you what we hold, and delete it. We will not require you to create an account in order to make this request, and there is no charge.

**Third-party services.** The App does not knowingly transmit any child's information to a third party. Where a service provider processes data on our behalf, it is bound by contract to use that data only to provide its service to us and is prohibited from using it for its own purposes.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as long as the law requires.

**Data stored on your device** is retained until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes the App's local data from that device. Note that a device backup made before uninstalling may still contain a copy; manage that through your device backup settings.

**Data held by us,** if any, is deleted within 30 days of a verified deletion request, except where we must retain records to comply with legal or accounting obligations.

To request deletion, email **support@nexomalabs.com** with the subject line "Data deletion request" and tell us which app you are writing about. We will confirm when the deletion is complete. There is no charge for this, and we will not ask you to create an account in order to make the request.

## 9. Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data. Data stored on your device is protected by the device's own operating system protections, including file-level encryption when a passcode is set. Any data transmitted to our systems is encrypted in transit using TLS.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for keeping your device and Apple Account secure.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
- Delete your information.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.
- Receive your information in a portable, machine-readable format.
- Withdraw a consent you previously gave.
- Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

Exercise any of these rights by emailing **support@nexomalabs.com**. We will verify your request and respond within the period required by applicable law, and in any case within 30 days. If we decline a request we will tell you why.

Residents of California may also designate an authorised agent to make a request on their behalf. Residents of the EU or UK have the right to lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority, though we ask that you contact us first so we can try to resolve the matter.

## 11. International Transfers

Nexoma Labs LLC operates from the United States. If you use the App from outside the United States and any information reaches our systems, it may be processed in the United States, where data protection law may differ from that of your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
