## Reporting a Problem

To help us fix an issue quickly, include as much of the following as you can:

- The app name and version number, shown in the app's Settings or About screen.
- Your device model and operating system version, for example "iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 18.4".
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
- The exact steps that reproduce the problem.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if the problem is visual.

Please do not include passwords or payment card details in a support email. We will never ask you for them.

## Common Questions

### How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and choose **Restore Purchases**. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that made them, so make sure you are signed in with that account.

### How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by Nexoma Labs. On your device, open **Settings**, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, select the subscription and choose **Cancel Subscription**. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the current period.

### How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>, sign in, and select the purchase. If Apple declines and you believe the charge was our error, email support@nexomalabs.com and we will look into it.

### How do I delete my data?

See the deletion section of our [Privacy Policy](https://www.nexomalabs.com/fitly/privacy). In short: deleting the app removes data stored on your device, and you can email support@nexomalabs.com from any address to request deletion of anything held on our side.

### I lost my data after changing devices. Can you recover it?

If the app stores data only on your device, we do not hold a copy and cannot recover it. Restoring from an iCloud or encrypted local backup made before the change is usually the only route. We recommend keeping device backups switched on.

### The app crashes on launch.

Try, in order: force-quit and reopen the app, restart the device, confirm the operating system is up to date, then delete and reinstall the app. Reinstalling removes locally stored data, so restore from a backup first if that data matters. If the crash persists, email support@nexomalabs.com with your device and OS version.

## Accessibility

We aim to support the accessibility features built into iOS, including Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion. If you hit an accessibility barrier in the app, email support@nexomalabs.com and tell us what you were trying to do. Accessibility reports go to the front of our queue.

## Security Reports

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, email admin@nexomalabs.com with the details and steps to reproduce. Please give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and fix the issue before disclosing it publicly. We will acknowledge your report within 5 business days.
