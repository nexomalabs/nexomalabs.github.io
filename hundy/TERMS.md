# Terms of use

_The agreement between you and Hundy._

**Last updated:** 2026-08-24

## What Hundy is

Hundy is a fitness tracking app that counts push-up repetitions using your device camera. It is provided for general fitness and training purposes.

## Hundy is not medical advice

Rep counts, quality scores and form cues are training aids, not clinical assessments. Hundy cannot diagnose an injury, judge whether an exercise is safe for you, or replace a qualified coach or clinician. Consult a professional before starting a new training programme, and stop if you feel pain.

## Train safely

You are responsible for your own safety and for your surroundings. Clear enough space around you, place your phone where it cannot be stepped on or knocked over, and do not train to the point where your form collapses. Never place a device where a fall could injure you.

## Accuracy

Hundy verifies repetitions from what the camera can see. Lighting, camera angle, clothing and body position all affect what it can detect, and it will sometimes miss a rep or count one you would have judged differently. Counts are provided as-is, without warranty of accuracy.

## Your data

Your workouts are stored only on your device. You are responsible for your own backups; if you delete the app, lose the device or clear its data, your history is gone and cannot be recovered by anyone.

## Acceptable use

Do not use Hundy to record or analyse anyone who has not agreed to it. Do not attempt to falsify verified rep counts, particularly once competitive features exist — the entire value of a camera-verified number is that it is honest.

## Changes

These terms may be updated as the app gains features. Material changes will be described in the app before they take effect.
