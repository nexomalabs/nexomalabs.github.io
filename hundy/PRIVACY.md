# Privacy policy

_Short, because there is very little to say._

**Last updated:** 2026-08-24

## What Hundy collects

Nothing leaves your device. Hundy has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service and no backend. There is no account, so there is nothing tied to your identity.

## Camera

Hundy asks for camera access so it can watch your push-ups. Frames are analysed on the device and released immediately; no image is saved to storage, no image is transmitted, and no video is ever recorded. Revoking camera access in your device settings stops rep counting and nothing else.

## What is stored on your device

Hundy keeps a local database containing only:

- Your workout sessions: date, duration, rep counts, quality scores and tempo.
- Per-rep detail: depth, tempo and which form rules a rep missed, for the history window you choose.
- Your settings, including your display name if you set one.

## What is never stored

No images, no video, no audio, no location, no contacts, no device identifiers and no advertising identifiers.

## Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled locally by your device. No push server is involved and no reminder data is sent anywhere.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Data and privacy → Delete all data removes everything immediately and irreversibly. Uninstalling Hundy removes the same data. There is no copy anywhere else to request or delete.

## If this ever changes

Optional features that need a server — cloud backup, leaderboards, friend challenges — will be exactly that: optional, off by default, and clearly described before you turn them on. On-device rep counting will never require an account.
